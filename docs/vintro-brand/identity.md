# Vintro — Brand Identity

**Brand idea**
The living memory of your car.

**Core promise**
Vintro remembers what happened to your car, understands its current state, and helps you know what it needs next.

**Tagline**
**Every car has a story. Vintro knows the story — and uses it to understand what the car needs next.**

**Positioning**
A digital vehicle record that turns service history into useful intelligence.

**Brand pillars**

* **Memory** — one complete vehicle history
* **Clarity** — understand what has been done and why
* **Foresight** — know what should happen next
* **Trust** — create a verifiable record that stays with the car

**Personality**
Intelligent. Calm. Precise. Trustworthy. Automotive, but not “petrolhead”.

**Tone of voice**
Simple, confident and helpful. Technical when necessary, never complicated.

**Visual direction**
Premium European automotive tech. Clean typography, generous spacing, restrained UI, strong vehicle photography and subtle references to timelines, records and diagnostics.

**Brand shorthand**
**Vintro = the memory and intelligence layer for your car.**
